The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, summoned (documents 20 & 21)
and heard from the driver and team representatives, have considered the following matter and
determine the following:

No / Driver    51 - Rafael Camara
Competitor TGR Haas F1 Team
Time           16:31
Session        Free Practice 2
Fact           Car 51 unnecessarily impeded Car 16 in turn 15.
Infringement Breach of Article B4.1.1 of the FIA F1 Regulations.
Decision       Driver: Reprimand (Driving).
               This is the driver’s 1st reprimand of the season.
Reason         The Stewards heard from the driver of Car 51 (Rafael Camara), team
               representatives and reviewed video, team radio and in-car video evidence and
               determined that Car 51 was warned multiple times that Car 16 (LEC) was arriving on
               a fast lap. Yet, Car 51 was weaving and moving well into the racing line. Car 16 had to
               take evasive action to avoid a collision by moving to the right, with a significant
               amount of the car off the track. The situation that was created was clearly dangerous
               and Car 51 should have taken more care to avoid impeding Car 16.
               While the typical penalty for impeding in a Free Practice session is a warning, in this
               instance, we are of the view that a reprimand is warranted.

               Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
               Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
               Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.


               Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
               on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
