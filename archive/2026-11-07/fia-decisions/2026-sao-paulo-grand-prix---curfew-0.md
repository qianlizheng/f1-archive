2026 SÃO PAULO GRAND PRIX
                                      06 - 08 November 2026


From         The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                      Document     27
To           The Stewards                                              Date         07 November 2026
                                                                       Time         09:03


                                    Technical Delegate’s Report



Last night team personnel of the Oracle Red Bull Racing team, who are associated with the
operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the eleven and a half (11.5) hour
period which commenced at 21:00 on 06 November, fourteen and a half (14.5) hours before the
scheduled start time of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 at 08:30
on 07 November.

This was the second (2nd) of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the Oracle Red Bull
Racing team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action should be
taken.



Last night team personnel of the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, who are associated with
the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the eleven and a half (11.5)
hour period which commenced at 21:00 on 06 November, fourteen and a half (14.5) hours
before the scheduled start time of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3
at 08:30 on 07 November.

This was the third (3rd) of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the Aston Martin Aramco
Formula One Team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action
should be taken.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
