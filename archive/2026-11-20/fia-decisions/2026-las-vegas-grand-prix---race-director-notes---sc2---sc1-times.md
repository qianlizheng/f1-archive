2026 LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX
                                       19 - 21 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Race Director                          Document     21
To          All Teams, All Officials                                 Date         19 November 2026
                                                                     Time         22:02


                                          Note to Teams
In order to ensure that cars are not driven unnecessarily slowly on any laps during and after the end
of Qualifying or during reconnaissance lap(s) when the pit exit is opened for the Race, drivers must
stay below 2:00.0 between the Safety Car lines shown on the pit lane drawing.




Rui Marques

The FIA Formula 1 Race Director
