2026 QATAR GRAND PRIX
                                     27 - 29 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                      Document     27
To          The Stewards                                              Date         28 November 2026
                                                                      Time         14:45


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



Last night team personnel of the McLaren Mastercard F1 Team and the Oracle Red Bull Racing
team, who are associated with the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during
the thirteen (13) hours and seventeen (17) minutes period which commenced at 01:13 on 28
November, sixteen (16) hours and seventeen (17) minutes before the scheduled start time
of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 at 14:30 on 28 November.

This was the third of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the McLaren Mastercard F1
Team and the Oracle Red Bull Racing team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season
and therefore no action should be taken.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
