2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                     30 October - 01 November


From          The Stewards                                             Document     32
To            The Team Manager,                                        Date         30 October 2026
              Audi Revolut F1 Team
                                                                       Time         20:49


The Stewards, having received a report from the Technical Delegate, summoned (document 30) and
heard from the team representative, have considered the following matter and determine the
following:

No / Driver     5 - Gabriel Bortoleto
Competitor Audi Revolut F1 Team
Time            16:29
Session         Free Practice 2
Fact            The floor of Car 5 was transported by team personnel from one side of the garage to
                the other whilst covered by a large sheet. In addition, team personnel obstructed the
                view of Car 5 using another sheet while mechanics were working underneath the car.
Infringement Breach of Article B3.3.1 of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.
Decision        The competitor (Audi Revolut F1 Team) is fined €5,000.
Reason          The Stewards heard from the Team Representative, the FIA Techincal Delegate and
                reviewed video evidence.
                The Team Representative recognised the breach and explained that the principal
                reason for the infringement was the particular configuration of the garages allocated
                to the Competitor at this Event. The two garages used by the Competitor are
                separated by a garage occupied by other personnel. As a result, the floor had to be
                transported through an area visible to third parties, which led the Competitor to cover
                it while moving it between its two garages.
                While the Stewards acknowledge the particular circumstances explained by the
                Competitor, these do not constitute an exception to the requirements of Article
                B3.3.1.
                Furthermore, team personnel also obstructed the view of Car 5 using another sheet
                while mechanics were working underneath the car.
                The Stewards therefore determine that the Competitor was in breach of Article B3.3.1
                of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.
                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.
                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
