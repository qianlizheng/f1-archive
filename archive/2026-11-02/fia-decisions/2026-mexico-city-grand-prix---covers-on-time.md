2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                       30 October - 01 November


From        The FIA Formula 1 Race Director                          Document    47
To          All Teams, All Officials                                 Date        31 October 2026
                                                                     Time        16:31


                                            Note to Teams
In accordance with Article B3.4.3a of the FIA F1 Regulations, all F1 Cars used during the Qualifying
Session must be covered and ready for FIA seals to be applied by 18:08.


Rui Marques

The FIA Formula 1 Race Director
