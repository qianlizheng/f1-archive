2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                    30 October - 01 November


From         The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                     Document   53
To           The Stewards                                             Date       31 October 2026
                                                                      Time       16:53


                                    Technical Delegate’s Report



Car 30, driver Liam Lawson, was in breach of the minimum MGU-K power requirement just after
crossing the line on his fastest lap in Q2 at 15:47:36.

This is a breach of Articles C5.12.6 and C5.12.7. The driver maximum power demand and the
electrical DC power of the ERS-K were reduced at greater rates than what is permitted by the
Regulations.

I am referring this matter to the stewards for their consideration.


Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
