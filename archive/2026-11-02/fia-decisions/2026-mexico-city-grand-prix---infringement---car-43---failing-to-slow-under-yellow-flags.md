2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                   30 October - 01 November


From          The Stewards                                              Document     85
To            The Team Manager,                                         Date         01 November 2026
              BWT Alpine F1 Team
                                                                        Time         19:35


The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, summoned (document 78) and
heard from the driver and team representative, have considered the following matter and determine
the following:

No / Driver    43 - Franco Colapinto
Competitor BWT Alpine F1 Team
Time           16:04
Session        Race
Fact           The driver passed through a single yellow flag sector and did not make the required
               reduction in speed.
Infringement Breach of Article B1.8.4a of the FIA F1 Regulations.
Decision       10 second time penalty imposed after the Race.
               1 penalty points (total of 5 for the 12 month period).
Reason         The Stewards heard from the driver of Car 43 (Franco Colapinto), team
               representative and reviewed positioning/marshalling system data, video, timing,
               telemetry, team radio and in-car video evidence.
               The driver and team presented telemetry and photographic evidence claiming it
               demonstrated that the driver had complied with the regulations which state that the
               driver must reduce speed and be prepared to change direction.
               A detailed examination of the telemetry and on board camera failed to satisfy the
               Stewards that the driver had sufficiently reduced speed through that single yellow
               sector.
               In relation to the previous double yellow flag sector the Stewards determined that
               there was sufficient doubt that the driver had sufficient warning in order to comply with
               the requirements, so did not determine that there was any breach in relation to that
               sector

               Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
               Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
               Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.

               Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
               on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
