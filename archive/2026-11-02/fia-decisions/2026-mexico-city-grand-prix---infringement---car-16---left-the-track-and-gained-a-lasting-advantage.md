2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                    30 October - 01 November


From          The Stewards                                             Document     69
To            The Team Manager,                                        Date         01 November 2026
              Scuderia Ferrari HP
                                                                       Time         14:42


The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following
matter and determine the following:

No / Driver     16 - Charles Leclerc
Competitor Scuderia Ferrari HP
Time            14:03
Session         Race
Fact            Car 16 left the track and gained a lasting advantage
Infringement Breach of Article B1.8.6 of the FIA F1 Regulations.
Decision        5 second time penalty.
Reason          The Stewards reviewed positioning/marshalling system data, video and in-car video
                evidence.
                The Stewards have determined that both cars entered turn 4 with excessive speed
                and therefore neither would have been able to make the corner on track, as
                demonstrated by the fact that car 44 which was on the inside, had to go off track to
                make the corner.
                Car 16 was therefore not entitled to claim the corner due to the excessive entry
                speed. The driver then completed an overtake whilst off track and accordingly this
                warrants a penalty.
                The baseline penalty guideline is 10 seconds however in mitigation is the fact that car
                44 was also off track on the inside of car 16 and this occurred early in the first lap.
                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.
                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
