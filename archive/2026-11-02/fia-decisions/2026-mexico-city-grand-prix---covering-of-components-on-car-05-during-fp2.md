2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                   30 October - 01 November


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                      Document     29
To          The Stewards                                              Date         30 October 2026
                                                                      Time         17:58


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



During FP2 the Audi Revolut F1 Team undertook some work on car number 05 in the battery pack
area. By doing so, the team had put a soft cover just behind the front axle blocking the view onto the
mechanics work.

Furthermore the team carried a floor, completely covered, through the pit-lane out of the garage and
back into the garage.

As all these actions are not in compliance with Article B3.3 of the 2026 Formula One Regulations, I
am referring this matter to the stewards for their consideration.


Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
