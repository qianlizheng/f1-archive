                                   30 October - 01 November


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                     Document     3
To          The Stewards                                             Date         28 October 2026
                                                                     Time         14:53


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



After the race in Austin, car number 63 was randomly chosen among the top ten cars for more
extensive physical inspections.

Subject to these physical inspections were the ICE air intake cooling system as well as all attached
sensors, their loom routing and connections to the SECU and other units.

All inspected components were found to be in conformance with the 2026 Formula One Technical
Regulations.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
