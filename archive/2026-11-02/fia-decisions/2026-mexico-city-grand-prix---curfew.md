2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                   30 October - 01 November


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                      Document    38
To          The Stewards                                              Date        31 October 2026
                                                                      Time        08:55


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



Last night team personnel of the Scuderia Ferrari HP team, who are associated with the operation of
the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the eleven and a half (11.5) hour period which
commenced at 21:00 on 30 October, fourteen and a half (14.5) hours before the scheduled start
time of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 at 08:30 on 31 October.

This was the third of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the Scuderia Ferrari HP team
during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action should be taken.



Last night team personnel of the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, who are associated with
the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the eleven and a half (11.5)
hour period which commenced at 21:00 on 30 October, fourteen and a half (14.5) hours before the
scheduled start time of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 at 08:30
on 31 October.

This was the second of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the Aston Martin Aramco
Formula One Team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action
should be taken.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
