2026 MEXICO CITY GRAND PRIX
                                        30 October - 01 November


From         The Stewards                                              Document      54
To           All Teams, All Officials                                  Date          31 October 2026
                                                                       Time          17:01


Communication from the Stewards

To all Teams and Drivers

Clarification of application of Driving Standards Guidelines

Applicable for this event, on Lap 1 and the first lap after any Resumption, in Turns 1 to 3, and Turns
4 to 6, all cars will, by default, be considered as both attacking and defending. Thus, in line with
existing guidelines, any car leaving the track in these areas and maintaining a position to a car or
cars within close proximity behind (as determined by the stewards) may be considered as gaining a
lasting advantage, unless such position is yielded by Turn 4 [in the case of a car leaving the track in
the area of Turn 1 to 3], or by Turn 12 [in the case of a car leaving the track in the area of Turns 4 to
6.]
