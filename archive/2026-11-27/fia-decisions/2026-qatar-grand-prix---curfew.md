2026 QATAR GRAND PRIX
                                      27 - 29 November 2026


From         The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                       Document     10
To           The Stewards                                               Date         27 November 2026
                                                                        Time         12:50


                                    Technical Delegate’s Report



Wednesday night team personnel of the BWT Alpine Formula One Team, who are associated with
the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the thirteen (13) hour period,
which commenced at 22:30 on 25 November, fourtytwo (42) hours before the scheduled start time
of P1 and ends twentynine (29) hours before the scheduled start time of P1 at 11:30 on 26
November.

This was the second (2nd) of the six (6) individual exceptions permitted for the BWT Alpine Formula
One Team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action should be
taken.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
