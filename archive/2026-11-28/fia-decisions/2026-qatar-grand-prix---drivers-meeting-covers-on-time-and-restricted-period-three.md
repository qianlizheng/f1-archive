2026 QATAR GRAND PRIX
                                       27 - 29 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Race Director                        Document    22
To          All Teams, All Officials                               Date        27 November 2026
                                                                   Time        21:48


                                          Note to Teams
Due to the extension of the FP2 session in accordance with Article B4.2.1 of the FIA F1
Regulations:
   - In accordance with Article B1.4.2 of the FIA F1 Regulations, the F1 Drivers' Meeting will take
   place at 22:43.
   - In accordance with Article B3.4.1a of the FIA F1 Regulations, all F1 Cars used during FP2 must
   be covered and ready for FIA seals to be applied by 00:13.
   - In accordance with Article B9.5.1c.i of the FIA F1 Regulations, Restricted Period Three will
   commence at 01:13.




Rui Marques

The FIA Formula 1 Race Director
