The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following
matter and determine the following:

No / Driver     27 - Nico Hulkenberg
Competitor Audi Revolut F1 Team
Time            13:20
Session         Reconnaissance Laps
Fact            Car 27 did not cross the pit exit white line at the earliest opportunity.
Infringement Breach of Article 12.2.1.i of the FIA International Sporting Code and non-compliance
             with Race Director’s Event Note (item 5.7, document 34).
Decision        Driver: Reprimand (Driving).
                This is the driver’s 3rd reprimand of the season.
Reason          The Stewards reviewed video evidence and determined that Car 27 which did not
                perform a practice start did not cross the white line separating the Pit Exit Road from
                the track at the earliest opportunity and join the “normal” racing line as required. This
                is a breach of the Race Director’s Competition Note.

                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.



                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
