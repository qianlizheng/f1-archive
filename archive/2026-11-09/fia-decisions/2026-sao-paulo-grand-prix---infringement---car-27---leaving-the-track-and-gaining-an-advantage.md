The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following
matter and determine the following:

No / Driver     27 - Nico Hulkenberg
Competitor Audi Revolut F1 Team
Time            15:09
Session         Race
Fact            Car 27 left the track and gained a lasting advantage.
Infringement Breaches of Article B1.8.6 of the FIA F1 Regulations and Appendix L, Chapter IV,
             Article 2 c) of the FIA International Sporting Code.
Decision        10 second time penalty.
Reason          The Stewards reviewed in-car video evidence and determined that Car 27 left the
                track while attempting to overtake Car 43 at Turn 1 on the outside. Car 27 then
                rejoined the track ahead of Car 43.
                Car 43 was ahead at the apex of Turn 1 and had the right to the corner per the
                Driving Standards Guidelines. Car 27 gained a lasting advantage by leaving the track.
                We imposed a 10 second time penalty on Car 27.

                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.



                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
