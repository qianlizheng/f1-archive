The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following
matter and determine the following:

No / Driver     44 - Lewis Hamilton
Competitor Scuderia Ferrari HP
Time            14:04
Session         Race
Fact            Car 44 allegedly left the track at turn 5 and gained a lasting advantage.
Infringement Alleged breaches of Article B1.8.6 of the FIA F1 Regulations and Appendix L,
             Chapter IV, Articles 2 c) of the FIA International Sporting Code.
Decision        No further action.
Reason          The Stewards reviewed in-car video evidence and determined that Car 44 was
                attempting an overtake on Car 87 at Turn 5. In defending the position, Car 87 moved
                to the right and Car 44 was pushed off the track to avoid a collision. Car 44 clearly
                had the momentum for the overtake and gained no advantage by going off the track.
                We also noted that the position was subsequently given back by Car 44. We
                therefore took no further action.

                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.


                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
