2026 LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX
                                     19 - 21 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                       Document     26
To          The Stewards                                               Date         20 November 2026
                                                                       Time         13:41


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



Last night team personnel of the Atlassian Williams F1 Team and the TGR Haas F1 Team, who are
associated with the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the twelve and
a half (12.5) hour period which commenced at 01:00 on 20 November, fifteen and a half (15.5)
hours before the scheduled start time of FP3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start
time of FP3 at 13:30 on 20 November.

This was the first of the four (4) individual exceptions permitted for the Atlassian Williams F1 Team
and the TGR Haas F1 Team during the 2026 Formula One Championship season and therefore no
action should be taken.




Jo Bauer

The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate
