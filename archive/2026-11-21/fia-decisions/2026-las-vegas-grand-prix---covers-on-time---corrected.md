2026 LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX
                                       19 - 21 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Race Director                          Document    43
To          All Teams, All Officials                                 Date        20 November 2026
                                                                     Time        21:41


                                          Note to Teams
In accordance with Article B3.4.3a of the FIA F1 Regulations, all F1 Cars used during the Qualifying
Session must be covered and ready for FIA seals to be applied by 23:02.


Rui Marques

The FIA Formula 1 Race Director
