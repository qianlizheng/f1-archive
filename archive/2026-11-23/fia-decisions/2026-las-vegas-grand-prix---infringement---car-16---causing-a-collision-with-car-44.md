2026 LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX
                                       19 - 21 November 2026


From          The Stewards                                             Document     60
To            The Team Manager,                                        Date         21 November 2026
              Scuderia Ferrari HP
                                                                       Time         22:08


The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following
matter and determine the following:

No / Driver     16 - Charles Leclerc
Competitor Scuderia Ferrari HP
Time            21:11
Session         Race
Fact            Car 16 collided with Car 44 at Turn 7.
Infringement Breach of Appendix L, Chapter IV, Article 2 d) of the FIA International Sporting Code.
Decision        10 second time penalty.
Reason          The Stewards reviewed video and in-car video evidence.
                Car 16 attempted to overtake Car 44 on the inside at Turn 7. The Stewards noted
                that Car 16 initiated the overtaking manoeuvre from a considerable distance behind
                Car 44, attempting to position his car on the inside.
                In assessing the incident, the Stewards also took into account the FIA Driving
                Standards Guidelines, in particular the criteria applicable to overtaking manoeuvres
                on the inside of a corner. The Stewards determined that the front axle of Car 16 was
                not at least alongside the mirror of Car 44 at the apex of the corner.
                The Stewards therefore determined that Car 16 had not earned the right to the corner
                at Turn 7 and was wholly to blame for the resulting collision.
                Accordingly, the Stewards imposed a 10-second time penalty.
                Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the
                Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and
                Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.

                Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely
                on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.
