2026 LAS VEGAS GRAND PRIX
                                     19 - 21 November 2026


From        The FIA Formula 1 Technical Delegate                 Document    52
To          The Stewards                                         Date        21 November 2026
                                                                 Time        18:50


                                   Technical Delegate’s Report



The following parts and parameters have been replaced / changed during the Parc Fermé yesterday
and today:

Car 81:      RHS gearbox differential output seal



Mercedes:

Car 63:      Rear wing assembly



Red Bull Racing RB Ford:

Car 03:      ICE water header tank assembly (including PRV)

Ferrari:

Car 16:      ES (previously used)
             CE (previously used)
             ANC (intercooler water pump, new)
             Parameter changes associated with the PU elements replacement

Car 44:      Floor assembly (excluding plank and skids)



Atlassian Williams Mercedes:
Car 23:       ICE water expansion tank including P/T sensor
               Parameter changes associated with the P/T sensor replacement

Car 55:       RHS rear brake drum



Racing Bulls RB Ford:

Car 30:       Fuel tank NRV
              Fuel tank PRV
              Fuel Tank air pump



Aston Martin Aramco Honda:

Car 18:       ICE water header tank PRV

Car 14:       Turbocharger housing fastener
              LHS front rim seal
              Cylinder #1 spark plug
              Ignition calibration parameters



Haas Ferrari:

Car 87:       Neutral finder battery unit



Audi:

Car 05:       Rear wing assembly



Cadillac Ferrari:

Car 11:         Driver’s seat

Car 77:       LHS rear damper potentiometer
              Parameter changes associated with the potentiometer replacement



All above parts have been replaced with the approval of the FIA technical delegate following a written
request from the team concerned, this being in accordance with Article B3.5.4 of the 2026 Formula
One Sporting Regulations.
